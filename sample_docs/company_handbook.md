# Acme Cloud Employee Handbook (Sample)

This is a fictional document included for trying out the RAG service.

## Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 to 17:30, Monday to Friday. Employees may work flexible hours
between 7:00 and 19:00 with their manager's approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00.

## Remote Work

Employees can work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements require approval
from the department head. A one-time home office allowance of 500 AUD is available for equipment.

## Leave

- Annual leave: 20 days per year, accrued monthly.
- Personal or sick leave: 10 days per year.
- Parental leave: 18 weeks paid for the primary carer and 4 weeks for the secondary carer.

Leave requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance where possible.

## Security Policy

All laptops must use full-disk encryption and a password manager. Multi-factor authentication is
mandatory for email, source control and cloud consoles. Security incidents must be reported to
security@acme.example within one hour of discovery.

## Expenses

Expenses under 100 AUD can be claimed with a receipt photo. Expenses over 100 AUD need manager
approval before purchase. Claims must be submitted within 30 days.
